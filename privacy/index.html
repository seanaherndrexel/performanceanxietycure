Privacy Policy

This site collects as little as possible.

Mailing list

If you join the mailing list, we store your email address, the first name you give (if any), the date and the page you signed up on. We use it only to send articles, book news and occasional offers about The Performance Anxiety Cure. We never sell or share it. Every email includes a way to unsubscribe, and you can ask to be removed at any time.

Purchases

Direct purchases are processed by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe and are never seen or stored by this site. Stripe shares your name, email and order details with us so we can deliver your purchase. Amazon purchases are handled entirely by Amazon under its own privacy policy.

Analytics and cookies

This site does not use advertising cookies or sell data. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Health information

We never ask for health information, and the mailing list does not record anything about your health.
